    Projects: VAT treatment

    Decides whether a project's payments are VATable sales (12/112 goes to output VAT) or non-VAT sales. Confirm each with the accounting firm: for a VAT-registered business a sale is VATable unless the law exempts it.

    Purchases: VAT invoices

    Tick a purchase only if you hold a VAT invoice or official receipt for it in Ritehome's name and TIN. Leave the VAT box blank to use 12/112 of the amount, or type the VAT printed on the invoice.
