Before your first clock-in

What is recorded. Each time you clock in or out: a photo of your face, your phone's location at that moment, and the time. Once, at the start: a reference photo and a face template made from it (128 numbers that describe your face; it can't be turned back into a photo).

What is not. Your location is never followed between punches, and your photos are not sent to any outside company. The face check runs on your own phone and in Ritehome's own database.

Why. To record working time for payroll, and to confirm it was you who clocked in.

Who sees it. You see your own times. Only the admin sees everyone's photos, locations and face-check results.

How long. Photos are deleted after 90 days. Times are kept as payroll records. You may ask the admin to see, correct or delete your records, as the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) provides.

Take your reference photo

One clear photo of your face, in good light, without a cap or mask. Every clock-in photo is compared with it. Only the admin can replace it later.
